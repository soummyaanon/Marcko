01 / The premise

Most editors treat sharing as an afterthought.

Speed without trust is a leak waiting to happen. Marcko is built the other way around — sharing is the boundary, not the loose end.

The problem

Convenience is being traded for trust.

Public links are guessable. Drafts leak through pastebins. Recipients keep copies forever. Teams choose between fast and safe — and pick neither.

Marcko's answer

Authenticated sharing. Encrypted at rest. Revocable.

Only signed-in users can mint share links. Content is encrypted before it touches storage. Recipients get the URL, you keep the kill switch — revoke anything, instantly, from history.
